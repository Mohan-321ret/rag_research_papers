1. Introduction
Enterprise knowledge bases change continuously as policies, products and
regulations evolve. Static retrieval systems answer from stale snapshots.

2. Drift-Aware Retrieval
We version every document and measure semantic distance between versions.
Retrieval penalizes chunks whose knowledge has drifted from the current state.

3. Evaluation
Experiments on enterprise corpora show improved answer freshness and
citation accuracy compared to conventional RAG baselines.
