1. Annual Leave
Annual leave is 25 days per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Sick leave is 15 days per calendar year.

3. Remote Work
Remote work requires written manager approval.

4. Expenses
Expense claims must be filed within 30 days.

5. Travel
Economy class is standard for all air travel.

6. Equipment
Laptops are refreshed every three years.

7. Training
Each employee receives a 1000 dollar training budget.

8. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid leave.

9. Overtime
Overtime is compensated at 1.5 times the base rate.

10. Probation
New joiners serve a six month probation period.

11. Notice Period
The standard notice period is one month.

12. Confidentiality
Confidential information includes customer records.
