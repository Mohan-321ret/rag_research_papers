1. Annual Leave Policy
Employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in
addition to public holidays. Unused days may be carried over into the
first quarter of the following year with manager approval.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

3. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to
manager approval and team coverage requirements.

4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental
leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.

5. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days with itemized
receipts attached to the expense report.
