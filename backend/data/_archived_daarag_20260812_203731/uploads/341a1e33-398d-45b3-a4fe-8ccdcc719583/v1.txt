1. Annual Leave Policy
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Up to 5 unused days may be
carried over into the first quarter of the following year. Leave requests
must be submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. A medical certificate is
required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

3. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.
