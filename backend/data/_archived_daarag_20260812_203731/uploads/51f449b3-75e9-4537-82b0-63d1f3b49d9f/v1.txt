1. Annual Leave Policy (POL-7734)
Full-time employees are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per
calendar year, in addition to public holidays. Requests must be
submitted through the HR portal at least two weeks in advance.

2. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to
manager approval and team coverage requirements.

3. Wellbeing Support
The leave policy is related to our wellbeing philosophy, which
emphasizes psychological safety and flexible scheduling across teams.
